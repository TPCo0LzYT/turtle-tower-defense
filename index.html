Questions

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The game is built for touch: tap a turtle, then tap the grass to place it. Phones and tablets also buzz when a fish slips by. On iPhone, open the game in Safari and use Share, then Add to Home Screen, for a full-screen icon.

What’s in the demo?

Two modes: Hatchling Mode and Save the Ocean Mode. The demo is coming soon.

How does the license key work?

After you buy, you get a license key. Enter it in the game to unlock the full version. One key works on 2 devices.

What if I change computers?

Deactivate the key on a device you no longer use, then enter it on the new one. If you get stuck, reach out to us and we’ll help.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. It’s $3.99, one time.
